How to play

Tilt mode: tilt your phone left and right to steer. Hold your thumb anywhere on the screen to go faster.

Touch mode: tap the left or right side of the screen to change lane. Hold to go faster.

Keyboard: arrow keys or A and D change lane. Hold Space to go faster.

Score more: grab coins and squeeze past cars. Close calls raise your multiplier. Sitting still in one spot won't save you for long.
